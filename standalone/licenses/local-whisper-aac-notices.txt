AAC-LC / ISO BMFF Python adaptation for hermes-standalone (2026)

The Python AAC spectral decoder, tables, PCE routing, AVTX-derived transform
and overlap, and MP4/ADTS demux logic are derived from pinned FFmpeg 8.1.2.
These adapted modules are licensed under GNU LGPL-2.1-or-later. The complete
license is retained in standalone/licenses/ffmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt. Original
copyright and license notices follow. The full corresponding Python source
is distributed in the standalone artifact. No native library or executable
is included or invoked at runtime. C/native authoring oracles are not runtime
dependencies. Tables and original native PCM fixtures are losslessly retained.
