are distributed with OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl, commit

The complete GNU GPLv3 license is in piper-gpl3.txt.

Nakdimon source/model: https://github.com/elazarg/nakdimon
Original Piper source records the upstream project in piper/hebrew/SOURCE.
Copyright 2022 Elazar Gershuni. The MIT text is preserved in
piper-nakdimon-mit.txt. The original model bytes and all character mappings
are retained without quantization or transcription substitution.

Arabic diacritization is Piper's Python port of libtashkeel:
https://github.com/mush42/libtashkeel
Its model is exactly the resource distributed by pinned Piper1.8.0;
the original numeric weights and three JSON maps are unchanged.
