Compiled dictionaries and phoneme data are included separately under
assets/piper/espeak-ng-data with their original resource notices. These
Python modules execute original lookup, rule, stress, spelling, morphology,
mnemonic conversion and IPA rendering algorithms. Their test fixtures are
expected original native results, not runtime word/sentence caches.
No native library, executable or third-party SDK namespace is shipped here.
