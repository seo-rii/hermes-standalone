Vorbis I / Ogg Python adaptation for hermes-standalone (2026)

The Python entropy codebooks, floor0/1, residue0/1/2, inverse coupling,
windows, overlap and Ogg Vorbis timing/history are derived from pinned
FFmpeg 8.1.2. These adapted modules are distributed under LGPL-2.1-or-later.
The original MIT notice for oggparsevorbis.c is retained below. The complete
LGPL text is in standalone/licenses/ffmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt. Full corresponding
Python source, all source-derived table words and notices ship in the bundle.
No native library, executable, installation or download is used at runtime.

Original pinned FFmpeg archive:
https://codeload.github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tar.gz/38b88335f99e76ed89ff3c93f877fdefce736c13
SHA256 2ae7e42343cfffb811d15cfe98b6d005f082595fcdf034d30a4ff90cfed9f9c6

The Xiph libraries are authoring-only encoders for independent generated
silence, tone and deterministic noise inputs. Their binaries and source are
not runtime payloads. Their notices are retained for the provenance of the
generated fixtures; no third-party audio was copied into these fixtures.
